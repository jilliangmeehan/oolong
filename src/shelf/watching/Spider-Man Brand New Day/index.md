This is the first Marvel movie I've seen in probably a decade. I did not see the other Tom Holland Spider-Man movies, but I got a very quick synopsis before it started, and that gave me enough context to not be confused about what was happening.

I had a good time! It was good! Not *super* good, but a fun way to spend a Sunday afternoon. It sort of made me want to catch up on all the Marvel movies I've missed, just so I know what's going on and all that. But I could also spend those hours doing a lot of other things, so idk.
