I first bought this game when I got my Nintendo Switch, because it was, at the time, one of the games everyone recommended for the Switch. I didn't appreciate turn-based RPGs at the time, so I found it boring and repetitive and quickly bounced off of it.

Now, all these years later, I *love* it. The combat is *fun* and the music is fantastic and the whole game is just beautiful to look at. The story isn't all that special, but it isn't bad, and I like seeing how things begin to fit together as I learn more about the world.
