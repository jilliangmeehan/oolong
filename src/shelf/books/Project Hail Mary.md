I saw [the movie](https://jillian.garden/shelf/watching/project-hail-mary/) before I read the book, which I think helped me like the book more. It was fun to read, to experience the story again, but in this case I liked the movie more — it kind of just works better in that format, where you can *see* what's happening and you know what Rocky looks and sounds like and the descriptions of the spaceship turning into a centrifuge or whatever aren't just going completely over your head every time.
